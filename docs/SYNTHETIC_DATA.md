## Generative decisions

### Population

- 100 synthetic users.
- Each user has between 1 and 300 simulated sleep nights.
- Calendar year: 2024.
- User IDs are artificial sequential integers.

### Sleep records

Each row represents one synthetic night of sleep:

- `sleep_id`: artificial key with format `user_id_YYYYMMDD`;
- `timestamp`: simulated sleep start between 21:00 and 23:59;
- sleep stages in seconds: `light`, `rem`, `deep`, `fall_asleep`, `awake`;
- `device`: simulated source device from `Source_A` to `Source_F`;
- `is_anomalous`: anomaly flag.

Total sleep duration is sampled between 14,400 and 36,000 seconds, equivalent to 4 to 10 hours.

Sleep-stage proportions:

- deep: 13% to 23%;
- REM: 20% to 25%;
- light: 45% to 55%;
- awake: 3% to 7%;
- fall-asleep time is the residual needed to match total sleep duration.

Anomalies:

- approximately 15% of sleep records are marked anomalous;
- anomalous sleep rows have missing sleep-stage values.

Duplicates:

- approximately 10% of sleep records are duplicated;
- duplicated rows use a different device and small perturbations in stage durations.

### Heart-rate records

The source generator creates daytime activity events associated with sleep records:

- 1 to 10 events per sleep record;
- events occur 6 to 24 hours before the sleep timestamp;
- duration is sampled between 300 and 7,200 seconds;
- activities include cycling, walking, running, swimming, yoga and gym.

Profile-dependent heart-rate ranges:

- sedentary: `hr_min` 60-70, `hr_avg` 70-90, `hr_max` 90-130;
- active: `hr_min` 40-55, `hr_avg` 55-75, `hr_max` 75-190.

Anomalies:

- approximately 15% of records receive implausible values;
- anomalous examples use low `hr_min`/`hr_avg` and `hr_max` above 200.

Duplicates:

- approximately 10% of heart-rate records are duplicated;
- duplicate records change the device and slightly perturb `hr_avg`.

### User demographics

The user dataset contains:

- `user_id`;
- `registered_at`;
- `age`;
- `gender`;
- `height_cm`;
- `weight_kg`;
- `bmi`;
- `activity_level`.

Age is sampled between 18 and 69. Height and weight are sampled from gender-conditioned normal distributions. BMI is computed from height and weight.

## Reproduction

From the repository root:

```bash
python3 scripts/generate_synthetic_sleep_hr.py --output-dir data/public/synthetic
```

This command writes:

- `simulated_sleep_data.csv`
- `simulated_heart_rate_data.csv`
- `simulated_user_data.csv`
